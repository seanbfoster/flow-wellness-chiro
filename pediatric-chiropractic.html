Pediatric chiropractic

Gentle care for growing kids.

Flow Wellness Chiropractic cares for children of all ages in Palm Beach Gardens, from infants to teens. Pediatric adjustments use gentle, age-appropriate techniques, tailored to support each child's natural growth and development.

Care led by Dr. Carter Matteson and Dr. Corey Matteson, Doctors of Chiropractic.

Why kids

Kids are often the ones who need chiropractic the most.

The nervous system coordinates and controls every organ, tissue, and cell in the body. Specific chiropractic care helps your child's spine support healthy communication between the brain and the rest of the body, so they can grow, move, and feel their best.

What to expect

A visit built around your child.

Every child is different, so care starts with understanding your child, never with a routine.

  1. I.

    A detailed history

    We start by learning about your child's health, development, and your goals as a parent, so we understand the whole picture.

  2. II.

    A careful exam

    Quick, gentle CLA INSiGHT™ scans look for areas of stress on your child's nervous system, so we know exactly where care is needed.

  3. III.

    Gentle adjustments

    Pediatric adjustments use gentle, age-appropriate techniques. Every adjustment is based on what we find, so your child gets only the care they need.

Common reasons for care

Why parents bring their kids in.

Families often visit a pediatric chiropractor for concerns like these.

  • Colic and fussiness
  • Trouble latching or nursing
  • Sleep troubles
  • Constipation and digestive issues
  • Head tilt or torticollis
  • Posture and sports injuries

Questions parents ask

Good questions, straight answers.

Do you see children?

Yes. Chiropractic care can be adapted for patients of all ages, from infants to seniors. Pediatric adjustments use gentle, age-appropriate techniques. Kids are often the ones who need chiropractic the most!

How does chiropractic care support nervous system function?

Chiropractic care focuses on identifying and correcting spinal dysfunction that may contribute to stress or dysfunction within the nervous system. This stress prevents the nervous system from providing efficient communication between your brain and the rest of your body. Through specific chiropractic adjustments, we aim to restore proper spinal motion and support healthy communication between the brain and body.
