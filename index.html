The Gonstead analysis

Our specific and individualized approach.

Using the Gonstead Technique, we take an individualized approach to every practice member through a thorough spinal and nervous system analysis. This allows us to deliver precise care based on what we find, not guesswork.

  1. I.

    Case history

    Every visit begins with a detailed health history. By understanding your health concerns, lifestyle, and goals, we gain valuable insight into your overall health and nervous system function.

  2. II.

    X-ray analysis

    When clinically appropriate, X-rays provide a clear view of the spine, allowing us to give the most precise and individualized adjustments. To see is to know and to not see is to guess.

  3. III.

    Instrumentation

    We use two tools to see what the nervous system is doing. The Nervoscope® and CLA INSiGHT™ are quick and gentle scans that look for areas of stress on the nervous system.

  4. IV.

    Visualization

    We carefully observe your posture and movement to identify patterns that may indicate imbalances or dysfunction.

  5. V.

    Palpation

    Through skilled hands-on assessment, we gently feel how your spine is moving and identify any stuck areas.

Frequently asked questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Do you see children?

Yes. Chiropractic care can be adapted for patients of all ages, from infants to seniors. Pediatric adjustments use gentle, age-appropriate techniques. Kids are often the ones who need chiropractic the most!

Will I be adjusted on my first visit?

Your chiropractor will first determine whether an adjustment is appropriate and safe based on your examination findings, or if additional examination, study, or referral is necessary.

Do I need to be in pain to benefit from chiropractic care?

No, many patients choose chiropractic care as part of a proactive wellness lifestyle. Because the nervous system controls every function in the body, maintaining proper spinal health can help support overall function and quality of life.
