Prenatal chiropractic

Care for every stage of pregnancy.

Chiropractic care during pregnancy helps support comfort, mobility, and pelvic balance for mom. At Flow Wellness Chiropractic in Palm Beach Gardens, every adjustment is modified to fit your stage of pregnancy.

For moms-to-be

Specific care for you and your little one.

We use specific adjustments tailored to moms-to-be and their little ones. Every pregnancy is different, so your care is based on a thorough history and exam, never a routine, and it adapts as your pregnancy progresses.

What prenatal care supports

Comfort, balance, and a healthy start.

Many moms-to-be come to us for concerns like these. Gentle adjustments, modified for each stage of pregnancy, may help ease these discomforts as your body changes.

  • Low back pain and sciatica
  • Pelvic pain and balance
  • Fatigue and low energy
  • Trouble sleeping
  • Heartburn
  • Mobility and comfort

Questions moms ask

Good questions, straight answers.

Do you care for pregnant women?

Yes. Chiropractic care during pregnancy helps support comfort, mobility, and pelvic balance for mom. Adjustments are modified to accommodate each stage of pregnancy.

Will I be adjusted on my first visit?

Your chiropractor will first determine whether an adjustment is appropriate and safe based on your examination findings, or if additional examination, study, or referral is necessary.

Do I need to be in pain to benefit from chiropractic care?

No, many patients choose chiropractic care as part of a proactive wellness lifestyle. Because the nervous system controls every function in the body, maintaining proper spinal health can help support overall function and quality of life.

Is the "popping" sound necessary for an adjustment?

No, the sound sometimes heard during an adjustment is simply gas being released from a joint. A successful adjustment does not depend on hearing a popping sound.
