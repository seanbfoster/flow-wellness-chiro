Meet the doctors

A husband-and-wife team, trained to be specific.

Dr. Carter and Dr. Corey Matteson both earned their Doctor of Chiropractic degrees at Palmer College of Chiropractic Florida. Former collegiate athletes, they share a deep appreciation for movement, precision, and helping the body perform at its best.

Doctor of Chiropractic

Dr. Carter Matteson

  • Palmer College of Chiropractic Florida
  • B.S., Florida Institute of Technology
  • Former collegiate swimmer

Dr. Carter earned his Bachelor's degree from Florida Institute of Technology before earning his Doctor of Chiropractic degree at Palmer College of Chiropractic Florida. As a former collegiate swimmer, he developed a deep appreciation for exercise, performance, and overall health.

Dr. Carter believes that through chiropractic care, the body is better able to heal, adapt, and thrive. He is committed to providing specific, individualized Gonstead chiropractic care to support each practice member's unique health goals.

Outside the office, he enjoys surfing, listening to music, and spending time outdoors. He looks forward to helping families in the Space Coast community experience the benefits of a healthier, better-functioning nervous system.

Doctor of Chiropractic

Dr. Corey Matteson

  • Palmer College of Chiropractic Florida
  • B.S., Eastern Michigan University
  • Former collegiate gymnast

Dr. Corey earned her Bachelor's degree from Eastern Michigan University before pursuing her Doctor of Chiropractic degree at Palmer College of Chiropractic Florida. As a former collegiate gymnast, she developed a passion for movement, precision, and helping the body perform at its best.

Passionate about helping others achieve their fullest health potential, she is dedicated to providing individualized, specific Gonstead chiropractic care tailored to each practice member's unique needs.

Outside the office, Dr. Corey enjoys surfing, dancing, and spending time outdoors. She looks forward to welcoming new practice members and serving the Space Coast community by helping individuals and families achieve their highest level of health through optimal nervous system function.
